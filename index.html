Our Story

An Unexpected Meeting

Add your photo assets/img/couple-photo.jpg

If you'd told us that a matched profile on a dating app would read like the opening line of an unlikely adventure, we might not have believed you.

For their first date, Marc picked a cozy European restaurant - a small nod to Naomie's roots across the Atlantic - and by the time the check arrived, neither of them wanted the evening to end. The conversation just kept going, the kind that makes you lose track of time entirely, and somewhere in there we both quietly knew this wasn't going to be just one date.

When it came time to propose, Marc went just as off-script - a themed escape room, down on one knee in the middle of a puzzle Naomie did not see coming. Two unlikely paths, one very lucky match - and we can't wait to celebrate with you.

Getting Here

The nearest airport is Colorado Springs Airport (COS), roughly a 25–35 minute drive to the Black Forest area depending on traffic. Denver International Airport (DEN) is a further option, about 1.5–2 hours north. A rental car is recommended, as the venue sits in a rural, wooded part of El Paso County.
