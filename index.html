For Those Who Travel Far

Travel & Accommodations

Getting Here

The nearest airport is Colorado Springs Airport (COS), roughly a 25–35 minute drive to the Black Forest area depending on traffic. Denver International Airport (DEN) is a further option, about 1.5–2 hours north. A rental car is recommended, as the venue sits in a rural, wooded part of El Paso County.

Where to Stay

No official room block yet — here are well-rated options near the venue, roughly ordered by distance:

Black Forest Lodge (B&B)

~$115–$160 / night

Rustic log lodge right in Black Forest — closest option to the venue, cozy country feel.

Hampton Inn Colorado Springs Northeast

~$100–$135 / night

Reliable, well-reviewed, free breakfast — about a 20 minute drive to the venue.

Drury Inn & Suites (near AFB)

~$90–$105 / night

Highly rated, good value, roughly 25 minutes from Black Forest.

Staybridge Suites NE Powers

~$120–$150 / night

Extended-stay suites, good for guests staying multiple nights.

Prices are estimates based on typical rates and will vary by season and how early you book. We recommend booking a few months out.
